# Al Rayyan Energy & Industrial Solutions

Official website for **Al Rayyan Energy & Industrial Solutions LLC** – a premier supply partner for EPC contractors and major energy operators across the UAE and Middle East.

## Overview
- **Structure**: Semantic HTML5 with accessibility standards (ARIA).
- **Styling**: Modern vanilla CSS with responsive clamp typography, strict 7-color brand palette, and fluid grid layouts.
- **Interactivity**: Clean, dependency-free vanilla JavaScript controllers.

## Key Sections
1. **Header & Navigation**: Brand logo, active state navigation, language selector, and quick CTA actions.
2. **Hero Section**: Expanding dynamic slider with right-side waiting strips and auto-slide rotation.
3. **Logo Carousel**: Continuous multi-brand partner ticker.
4. **About Us**: 14+ years experience badge, mission details, and industrial highlights.
5. **Interactive Flange Visualizer**: Scroll-reactive SVG transformation.
6. **Our Services Slider**: 2-slide grid showcasing 8 core oil & gas supply domains.
7. **Client Showcase**: Trusted global energy operator logos.
8. **Why Choose Us**: Dual-column capability breakdown with industrial machinery graphics.
9. **Commitment to Quality**: ISO/API/ASME compliance, HSE protocols, and sustainability practices.
10. **Testimonials Slider**: Verified client testimonials with hover states and carousel navigation.
11. **FAQ Section**: Smooth expanding/collapsing accordion answers.
12. **Request a Quote**: Lead capture form with product selector and direct contact inquiry.
13. **Footer**: Quick links, corporate info, and social connections.
